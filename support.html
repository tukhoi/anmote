Anmote Support Anmote is a remote control app for Android TV and Google TV devices, by Davang Solutions. GETTING STARTED 1. Open Anmote and allow local network access when prompted. 2. Your Android TV should appear under "Available Devices" automatically. 3. Tap it, then enter the code shown on your TV screen to pair. 4. Once paired, tap the device any time to reconnect instantly. WAKE-ON-LAN (OPTIONAL) To turn your TV on from a fully powered-off state (not just standby), long-press a paired device and choose "Enable Wake From Off," then enter the TV's Wi-Fi MAC address (found in the TV's network settings). TROUBLESHOOTING - TV not appearing? Make sure your iPhone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network, and that the TV is powered on. - Connection fails? Try forgetting the device (swipe left on it) and pairing again. CONTACT ntkhoi@gmail.com
